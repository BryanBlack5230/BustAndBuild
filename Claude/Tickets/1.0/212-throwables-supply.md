## What to build

The throwable set grows to **3–4** (tar barrel exists) and the **supply specialty building produces them** — the city→battle production chain: staffed supply building produces stock over time (rate-based per the HS income model; hauling folds in when 214 lands) → racks near the Beacon refill → grab → throw → effect. Design-first: short spec per throwable.
